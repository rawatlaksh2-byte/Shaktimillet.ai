# Shakti Millets YouTube Brand Rules

## Non-negotiables

- Brand: **Shakti Millets**
- Tagline: **Ghar Ki Thali Shakti Wali**
- Use the latest approved Shakti Millets logo and packaging references.
- Do not redesign the logo, pouch shape, label system, seals or product identity.
- Keep millet grains realistic and visually distinguish processing types when the source material supports it.
- Use Hindi/Hinglish narration by default.
- Use a strong hook in the opening 2–3 seconds.
- Prefer premium photorealistic food visuals, real Indian-kitchen context, macro grain shots and natural textures.
- Default Shorts framing: 9:16.
- CTA can use Follow / Save / DM SHOP / DM PRICE only when relevant to the actual offer/workflow.

## Product scope

Primary millet/pulse content may cover approved SKUs including Kodo, Barnyard, Little, Foxtail, Proso, Browntop, Ragi/Finger Millet, Bajra/Pearl Millet, White Sorghum, Red Sorghum and Horsegram. Processing labels such as unpolished, semipolished and parboiled must match the actual SKU.

## Factual-safety rules

- No disease-treatment, cure, guaranteed weight-loss, diabetes-control or other medical promises.
- Nutrition claims need a reliable source and review.
- Do not invent current price, stock, delivery time, certification or discount.
- Do not imply a product is organic/chemical-free/certified unless that exact claim is currently documented for the SKU.
- Any factual claim that cannot be verified should remain blocked for human review.
- Uploaded third-party media must have confirmed usage rights.

## Publishing rule

Keep automatic publishing **OFF** initially. Draft → factual review → brand review → rights review → approve → schedule/publish.
